# Billing is deferred

LawRelay is a free beta. Keep `LAWRELAY_RELEASE_MODE=beta` and `LAWRELAY_BILLING_ENFORCED=true` in production. Verified members can post without a subscription; checkout and payment processing stay disabled.

Lawyers agree and settle their professional fees directly. After acceptance, they exchange invoice-recipient and Peppol details privately. LawRelay no longer collects member company or invoice details. Internal workspaces remain for permissions and verification.

Legacy billing structures remain inactive; they are not a reason to collect new company data. `Privacy::CompanyDataCleanup` clears unnecessary old fields while preserving recorded holds and historical financial evidence. See the [data register](data-register.md) and [retired KBO lookup note](kbo-lookup.md).

The [former pricing idea](../archive/pricing.md) is archived for reference. It is not a launch plan.
