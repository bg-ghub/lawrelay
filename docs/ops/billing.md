# Billing is deferred

LawRelay is a free beta. Keep `LAWRELAY_RELEASE_MODE=beta` and `LAWRELAY_BILLING_ENFORCED=true` in production. Verified firms can post without a subscription; checkout and payment processing stay disabled.

Lawyers agree and settle their professional fees directly.

The [former pricing idea](../archive/pricing.md) is archived for reference. It is not a launch plan.
