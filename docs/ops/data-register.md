The public privacy notice distinguishes Migadu's Swiss company location from its stated French email infrastructure. It does not claim all email-related processing is confined to France. `advocaat.be` is the public verification source, not an outsourced LawRelay hosting service; the app fetches the supplied profile to compare the name and published professional email.

At the applicant's request, signup fetches one public profile to suggest name, listed email, languages and practice areas. A signed, URL/name-bound preview is valid for 20 minutes and contains only the source URL, name and professional preferences; email remains an unverified form field. It creates no account or mail, and grants no verification; the normal fresh directory and email checks remain required after registration. The preview is held in the form, without persistent browser storage or a new provider. Office, address, phone and bank details are not imported.

Member company and billing collection ended in 0.19.0: signup, settings and assignments no longer request a company name, enterprise/VAT number, invoice recipient, billing address or Peppol details. After acceptance, the lawyers exchange invoicing and Peppol details privately. The operator's own legal notice and contact details remain public.

The internal `Firm` record is a neutral workspace for memberships, permissions and verification. Its identifiers and existing relationships remain; it no longer represents a collected legal company identity. Workspace members can still see proposals submitted to that workspace.

The production inventory on 31 October 2026 contained two workspaces and no assignments, invoices, payments, subscriptions or billing accounts. `Privacy::CompanyDataCleanup` clears unnecessary legacy company and invoice fields at application startup and during daily cleanup. Recorded holds and historical financial evidence protect the relevant records. Restored copies undergo the same cleanup before traffic resumes; ordinary backup retention still applies.

User URLs use a random, immutable UUID, separate from the internal database key. It is an account identifier, included in the member's access export; it grants no access. Admin authorization, fresh MFA and existing review scopes apply before lookup.

The server checks registered members' public advocaat.be profiles daily. Monitoring stores a target digest, account/profile reference, categorical result and timestamps, separate from access-verification evidence. It copies no response HTML or additional contact data. Missing or changed directory profiles produce one operator summary through the existing Migadu transport; outages remain indeterminate. Accounts are not suspended automatically. There is no active KBO monitoring source.

Latest monitoring state stays with the relevant account. Completed check summaries and resolved alerts are removed after 90 days; pending/failed delivery investigations remain open. Cleanup removes retired KBO targets and summaries. Account erasure removes obsolete directory targets and their summary references. Member access exports include their own directory-check status.

An inaccessible-email request stores the working address, request/decision times, reviewer and identity-check note on the professional profile. The address must be confirmed before an MFA-authenticated administrator approves it using independent professional contact details. Approval is bound to the current account and directory identity, and does not claim control of the old mailbox. Decision mail uses the existing Migadu transport. Member exports include review status, address and dates; internal review notes and reviewer details need separate assessment. Profile erasure removes these fields and scrubs its audit metadata.

The private archive was completed on 30 October 2026, with source URLs, retrieval times and checksums. Public references, the existing account DPA and local account observations are labelled separately; some original signup acceptance dates and historical versions remain unavailable. No new agreement or account setting was submitted.

Signup records acceptance of the terms and acknowledgement of the notice, rather than consent to all processing. ## Retention schedule

| Data | Current control and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Signup profile preview | Signed form data expires after 20 minutes; no preview table or persistent browser storage. Normal technical-log retention still applies. |
| Unconfirmed registration | Delete an isolated, never-activated signup after 30 days of inactivity. Preserve established accounts awaiting email reconfirmation, recent sessions/corrections, shared workspaces, work, financial evidence and protected requests. |
| Retired member company and invoice fields | Clear at startup and daily unless a recorded hold or historical financial evidence protects the relevant records. Keep the neutral workspace and relationships. Retired KBO monitoring records are removed. Protected backups follow the schedule below. |
| Active account and professional profile | Keep until closure, including the current email-access review and identity-check note. Profile erasure removes these fields. Audit history follows the one-year audit schedule. |
| Closed assignments, proposals and shared workflow records | Keep for 2 years after closure. Pseudonymise shared work when an account is removed where possible; do not promise anonymisation. |
| Notifications | Delete terminal notifications and delivery metadata after 90 days. Keep unresolved delivery investigations or held records until the review is finished. |
| Resolved reports, privacy requests and support correspondence | Keep for 2 years after resolution or last correspondence. The daily Migadu IMAP cleanup removes eligible `Support/Resolved` conversations and linked Sent copies; open, held, flagged, draft and unrelated mail stays protected. Closure timestamps are private metadata included in encrypted backups. |
| Audit and security records | Keep for 1 year. |
| Deletion ledger | Keep the minimum replay record for 2 years. It contains no email address or credentials and stops erased data returning after a restore. |
| Authentication sessions | Expire after 30 days; expired sessions are removed on access and by daily cleanup. Optional member two-factor approval lasts for that session; a new sign-in needs another code. Admin MFA authorisation lasts 12 hours. This does not delete account or audit data. |
| Optional two-factor credentials | Setup and active authenticator keys are encrypted. Ten recovery codes are shown once and stored as hashes; use removes a code. Turning two-factor off clears these credentials and signs out other sessions. Password resets preserve two-factor protection. |
| Background jobs | Hourly cleanup removes finished jobs older than the installed Solid Queue default of one day. Failed/pending jobs remain for investigation; they need operational review rather than a claimed one-day deletion guarantee. |
| Public-page analytics | Cookieless Umami runs automatically for anonymous public pages only. A first-party Statistics preference cookie remembers the visitor's setting for 180 days, and the footer control can turn statistics off or back on. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control block analytics. The information-banner dismissal cookie lasts 180 days; Umami itself uses no cookies. The LawRelay Umami database is cleaned daily; analytics records older than 90 days are removed. It is isolated from the other products. |
| Application and proxy logs | Keep for up to 31 days. Sensitive token paths are filtered, but logs can still contain account/request metadata. |
| Off-host backups | Keep restic snapshots for 30 days. AWS S3 current objects do not expire through lifecycle rules; noncurrent versions expire after 30 days. Deleted data can therefore remain in protected backup storage for about 60 days. Reapply approved deletion and retired-company cleanup before restored traffic resumes. |
| Hetzner provider backups | Separate daily disk-backup rotation with seven slots under the provider's [backup policy](https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/servers/backups-snapshots/overview/). These may include databases, logs and protected configuration files; treat access as production-level access. They are separate from restic retention. |
| GitHub records | Source/history, issues, CI logs and artifacts follow repository/provider settings. This register does not establish a uniform deletion duration. Keep real user evidence out of this channel. |

An unresolved privacy request, security incident, legal obligation or specific recorded dispute hold pauses deletion only for the necessary records. Review each hold at least every 90 days. Implementation references: `app/models/session.rb`, `app/models/user.rb`, `config/recurring.yml`, `compose.production.yml`, `script/ops/lawrelay-backup` and the protected provider configuration.

## Operator decisions

- Confirm the published legal name, enterprise/VAT details, registered address, platform role and contact ownership against the operating person and current records.
- Keep the private provider archive current when terms or service use change. Preserve the distinction between account agreements, public references and local observations.
- Approve the actual data purposes and eligibility rule: registration currently depends on `advocaat.be`, not a complete combined Belgian bar-directory service. Review the public terms/privacy text in NL/FR/DE/EN with that boundary in mind.
- Apply the retention schedule above, including the 90-day review of any recorded hold. Shared records are pseudonymised where possible; do not delete or rewrite another lawyer's work without review.
- Keep deletion restrictions with the private request record and reapply them after a restore. Do not promise immediate removal from protected backups.
- Validate identity checks, export redaction, deletion execution, complaints/appeals and incident escalation using the [support playbook](support.md). Record decisions separately from evidence that an action was carried out.
- Review email capacity before increasing traffic: Migadu Micro's [published quota](https://www.migadu.com/pricing/) is 20 outgoing attempts per day across the account. Verification, proposal notifications, alerts and support share it. This infrastructure limit does not cap open registration eligibility. Failed mail jobs trigger the existing health-alert route, but SMTP alert delivery shares the quota; keep smoke tests minimal and inspect the protected journal when delivery fails.
- Review the published notices and the decisions above. Outside specialist reviews are optional future work, not a paid requirement for this free beta. This register records engineering evidence and introduces no paid-subscription processing.
