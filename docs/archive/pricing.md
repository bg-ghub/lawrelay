# Archived pricing idea

Archived 29 October 2026. Reference only; there is no commitment or timetable for paid plans.

LawRelay is a free beta. Links to the operator's other products may help support hosting.

## Former proposal

- LawRelay Office: €49 per firm per month, excluding VAT.
- Manual Belgian B2B invoices, external Peppol delivery and bank reconciliation.
- Access after an administrator records cleared payment; monthly cancellation and expiry controls.
- Lawyers agree and settle assignment fees directly. LawRelay takes no commission.

The repository retains billing models and services. They are disabled in beta. Mollie webhook processing exists, but hosted checkout, automated invoicing and bank reconciliation are incomplete.

Any decision to charge would need fresh product, legal and accounting review, clear published terms, and tested payment, cancellation, refund and support flows. None of that is a beta requirement.
